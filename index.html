Material Risk and Shortage Prevention

Can a project move earlier, and do we have the material, the containers and the shop to do it?

Demo
1Standard
Material Risk and Shortage Prevention Agent
  • Watches long-lead and critical parts
  • Flags shortage risk weeks before production
  • Recommends fixes to the planner
2What we heard
A pull-in question takes hours to answer
  • 1
    Pull-ins take hours.Moving a project earlier means checking material on hand, material coming and shop capacity.
  • 2
    Containers by hand.Container buying works from backlog, on-hand and on-order reports.
  • 3
    Freight is hard to see.Ocean freight is hard to trace today.
  • 4
    Labor sets capacity.Capacity is planned by labor, and pushing an order out is an option too.
3Real in your tenant
Schedule change, Material and containers, Shop load
  • Ranks projects by weeks to the first shortage
  • Shows item coverage and a 10-week look-ahead
  • Shows order lines that land after the need date, and shop load by week
  • The assistant reruns the check at a new date; a person decides in LN
Reads only. Containers show as an example until a carrier feed is connected.

Project Margin

Which projects are losing margin, and why, while they are still running?

Demo
1Standard
Project Margin Agent
  • Compares baseline and forecast margin across projects and contracts
  • Uses cost forecasts and estimates at completion
2What we heard
Margin shows up too late
  • 1
    Margin shows late.Project managers see true profitability only after a project closes.
  • 2
    A top KPI.Margin should lead the dashboard, with issues visible while the project runs.
  • 3
    Hours off budget.Flag hours running at 2 times or half their budget.
  • 4
    Blurred costs.Outside fabrication and freight blur the cost picture until job close.
3Real in your tenant
Portfolio and Cost breakdown
  • Ranks active projects by margin points lost against the margin they were won at
  • Flags activities at 2 times or half their budgeted hours
  • Shows 8 weeks of planned shop hours

Cash Application and Collections

Match payments to invoices, and rank what to collect first.

Demo
1Standard
Accounts Receivable Agent
  • Tracks receivables, invoices, payments and customer credit
  • Answers through the GenAI Assistant
2What we heard
Payments and reminders run by hand
  • 1
    One payment, many invoices.One payment can cover several invoices, sometimes with no invoice reference.
  • 2
    A set order.Start with invoices 30 or more days past due, then reminder 1, 2 and 3, then escalation.
  • 3
    Retention.Retention is withheld on some invoices.
  • 4
    Show the work.People want to see the agent working.
3Real in your tenant
Cash application, Work queue, Receivables
  • Lists receipts LN didn't match, and suggests an invoice only on an exact, single match
  • A person approves the match in LN
  • Ranks invoices 30 or more days past due and names the next reminder
  • Shows aging and the customers with the most past due
